When you know you know

I went to get a haircut today and my barber and I started talking about love. We talked about the universe and people: how he met his wife and what led to it. How, when he met her, she took her sunglasses off and he knew.

When you know you know.

It’s always a beautiful story when someone tells you about them meeting their significant other, typically filled with joy, spontaneity and a little bit of the universe—or of God, depending on what you believe. But frankly there’s so much more to it, there’s a lot that builds the relationship that it feels like we forget. We always ask how’d you guys meet, not how you guys got here or what was the struggle that helped define your relationship.

More often than not, whenever we hear the origin stories people always say ‘And I just knew’ or “When you know you know” and my reaction is always… no… what does that mean? Help me understand it. Some people explain it eloquently and some people stumble through it but I think both sides are complete nonsense.

I don’t believe in the idea of “When you know, you know.”

I think when people say that about their SO, it’s them remembering the limerence: the extraordinary feeling of being wanted intimately by another human. Which is beautiful but not always true. I believe they remember the intense and beautiful feelings that are not of marriage. The fire that was lit by the spark, not actual having the ‘when you know you know’ moment.

I haven’t been in my fair share of relationships but the few short ones I have been in, I’m realizing that I fall victim to that feeling where I romanticize the person: that’s not bad, it’s normal. But when we explain meeting it’s wise for us to avoid the ‘deadly phrase’. Maybe, this phrase was built into us by Hollywood and the rest of society saying it should be magical. We should all have a fairytale and if you don’t then it’s not right: bullshit, in my honest opinion.

All that said, I do think there is that “When you know you know” moment. I believe that moment is built over months, years, and decades of growth with one another to drive towards intimacy. That moment is made when we choose to marry the person because at that point, you know they’re the one you want to spend the rest of your life with. For some people that does happen early, for others it takes longer and that’s ok. Maybe it’s all relative. In writing this, maybe there is such a feeling and maybe I haven’t felt it and that’s why I don’t know - yet.

I think there are other things to think about.

I’m sure there’s a ton of people out there who get this feeling in other relationships and it doesn’t work out. So that feeling of ‘when you know you know’ doesn’t work. But when it does work then we get the ‘I knew it when I saw her.’ Yea no shit you did, but you also thought that with everyone.

I don’t know.

It’s a fun and cool topic though that I don’t believe in :)
