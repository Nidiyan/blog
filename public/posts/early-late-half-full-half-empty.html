early, late, half full, half empty

I’m up tonight.

It’s really early, or maybe it’s late. Maybe it’s half full or maybe it’s half empty. I want it to be early because, to me, that’s half full and that’s who I am.

I feel alone. tired. exhausted. restless. anxious.

The Bible says to cast your anxieties on Him because he cares for you — 1 Peter 5:7. It says his yoke is easy and his burden is light — Matthew 11:30.

I don’t know.

Maybe I don’t know what it’s like to cast my anxieties, maybe I don’t know how to take up his yoke. Maybe this life wasn’t meant to be easy.

I want to feel accompanied. energetic. rested. peaceful. grounded.

But I don’t.

What am I supposed to do, Jesus? Tell me. The Bible says to do these things, but how do I do it. Am I supposed to cry out to you and beg for this. Am I supposed to be so far beaten that I don’t know what else to do. Help me.

I’m begging for you. I’m begging for your yolk: I want to cast my anxieties on you.

But I don’t know how.

Maybe this is how.
