where to go

i’m not sure what to write about.
my brain is searching and searching but emptiness.
I have an urge to pen these thoughts.

maybe there is nothing; maybe i don’t need to know.
I have no map, no destination.
I am free.

I choose where to go even when I don’t know.
follow the stream, cross the bridge, and wander.
keep wandering.

In the end, where I go is where i’m meant to be.
there is no plan, no purpose other than that.
my destination is right here.
