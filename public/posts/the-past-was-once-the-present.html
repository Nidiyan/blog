the past and the present

I have a lot of nostalgia for the past, a longing to be back again—to be present in a time where life was simpler and easier.

I feel like I have a lot of regrets, not in a debilitating or shameful way, but in a way that makes me think about life and what I’m doing now. If I did this or that, what would my career be, what would life be like? Would I be married or single, living alone or with another? Would I be permanently injured or in the best shape of my life.

FYI - I am in the best shape of my life.

Whenever I think about the past, though, a lot of it turns into a wish I did that or I should have and then I spiral and imagine—almost always it’s a fantasy about how much better my life could be—sometimes it isn’t.

In always thinking about the past I seem to think I can’t change and that the past is gone. While that’s true, I think another thing I’ve recently realized is that the past was once the present and we had a choice to make whatever decision we hope we made.

If we could time travel back to whenever, would we really change anything? Would we make that decision, take that action, maybe, I don’t know. If we would, then maybe this should cause us to reframe our minds. Let’s look at the present as if it were the past. What if, right now, you made that decision to take action; once you get to the end of your life, it isn’t something that you regret but it’s something you’re happy about, something you’re glad you did.

Not all of life needs to be this way, but maybe some of it should. Maybe we should think more and be active. Maybe maybe maybe.

Because sometimes when we say “I wish I did” we can actually say “I’ll do it right now”.
